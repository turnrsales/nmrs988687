We cannot expect people to naturally develop respect for laws and legal order unless they are guided to understand why those laws exist. Respect must be taught through clarity, fairness, and proper communication of legal responsibilities. Only when citizens see the value and purpose behind rules do they willingly follow them. This responsibility begins with those who enforce the law, because they set the example through their conduct and integrity. When we teach respect to the enforcers of justice, they in turn inspire society to honour the laws they uphold.
